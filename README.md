# SlickLab CommerceOS

**Human-Supervised AI Operations Platform for Online Sellers**

SlickLab CommerceOS helps Philippine merchants organize products, orders, inventory, customers, content, analytics, approvals, and business workflows in one controlled system.

**TindaBot** is the unified AI operations assistant inside CommerceOS. It prepares repetitive work, summarizes priorities, and produces evidence-backed recommendations while merchants retain control over product publishing, prices, refunds, purchases, supplier orders, and other business-critical decisions.

## Product Vision

Make advanced commerce operations accessible to every Philippine merchant, regardless of technical skill, team size, or sales channel.

## Product Mission

Connect the daily work of online sellers by replacing disconnected spreadsheets, inboxes, channel dashboards, and manual processes with one governed merchant command center.

## Public Preview

This repository is a source-available public preview. It may include a sanitized product demonstration and high-level architecture, but it intentionally excludes proprietary internal prompts, agent instructions, scoring logic, private retrieval content, detailed tool contracts, orchestration methods, operational runbooks, secrets, credentials, and customer data.

## Core Capabilities

- Merchant workspaces and role-based access
- Product catalog and CSV import/export
- Order and inventory management
- Customer directory and communication history
- AI-assisted product-content drafts
- Approval-controlled publishing and actions
- Business dashboards and daily action reports
- Audit trails and evidence-backed recommendations
- Approved marketplace, website, social, payment, and logistics adapters where available
