# Dependency and License Policy

Before adding a dependency:
1. Record project, exact version, source, license, copyright notice, and intended use.
2. Classify use as linked library, modified source, separate service, internal tool, or customer-facing managed capability.
3. Prefer permissive licenses for embedded product components.
4. Require review for AGPL, SSPL, BSL, Elastic, fair-code, source-available, community-edition, or proprietary terms.
5. Preserve required notices and generate an SBOM for every release.
6. Re-check licenses during major upgrades because licensing can change.
7. Do not copy third-party branding or imply endorsement.
